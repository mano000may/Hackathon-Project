    The Models

    Model status, live from the server

    1 · Data inTen years of prices and four macro series become 20 numbers per stock per day: momentum, trend, volatility, strength versus peers, and the rate backdrop.
    2 · Model runsTwo simple models, trained on every stock together: one estimates the return over the next five trading days, the other forecasts how large each stock’s moves will be. Simple on purpose, because simple models overfit less.
    3 · Signal outEstimates become a ranking and a risk rating. The report card tests both on two unseen years and says plainly where there is skill (risk) and where there isn’t (returns).

        How to Read This

        • Risk forecast is a separate model. Its job is the size of a stock’s moves, not their direction. It is scored against “volatility stays as it was lately”.
        • Direction accuracy is how often the predicted move had the right sign. Compare it with “always guess up”, the free baseline.
        • Rank skill (IC) checks, week by week, whether stocks the model ranked higher really did better than stocks it ranked lower. Zero is pure chance.
        • Best fifth vs. worst fifth is the average weekly gap between the stocks ranked highest and lowest. The t value says whether that gap is bigger than luck: above 2 is clear, near 0 is noise. Costs are not included.
